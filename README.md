# PEKS

## Regulatory Knowledge Infrastructure for Medical Device Development

PEKS explores a knowledge-driven approach to medical device development and regulatory documentation.

> **The hardest part of medical device automation is not generating documents. It is maintaining the knowledge, relationships, evidence, and traceability behind those documents.**

---

## Why PEKS

Medical device development produces interconnected information across:

- Product and design
- Risk management
- Risk controls
- Verification and validation
- Evidence
- Regulatory documentation

These elements are rarely isolated.

A change in one engineering decision may affect multiple risks, controls, verification activities, evidence records, and regulatory documents.

PEKS explores how these relationships can be structured, connected, traced, and reused as development knowledge.
